Where the idea started

Looking beyond the familiar.

Travel websites often lead us towards the same well-known destinations. But across India, there are smaller towns, traditional crafts, unusual landscapes and historic neighbourhoods that have their own stories to tell.

Hidden Gems is a way to bring some of those places into one accessible collection, making it easier to explore India through its local character, culture and history.

01 / The purpose

Discovery should be about more than a destination.

A place becomes memorable through the details that make it different. It might be the way a building was constructed, the story behind a local textile, the history of a trading town or the landscape that shapes everyday life.

The purpose of this project is to introduce visitors to lesser-known destinations and give them a starting point for learning more. Each entry brings together a location, a short introduction and a cultural or historical detail.

The website is designed as a simple discovery tool. Visitors can search for places, filter destinations by state or theme, save favourites and open individual stories.

What guides the archive

Three things
we care about.

The principles behind the way destinations are presented.

01 / CURIOSITY

Look closer

Go beyond a place name. Explore the traditions, landscapes, materials and everyday details that give a destination its identity.

02 / CONTEXT

Tell the story

Share useful context about a place instead of treating destinations as names on a checklist. History and local culture matter as much as scenery.

03 / ACCESS

Keep it simple

Make discovery straightforward with a clean interface, searchable entries, practical filters and saved places visitors can return to.

02 / Behind the website

A small project, built step by step.

Hidden Gems is a frontend web development project created using HTML, CSS, Bootstrap and vanilla JavaScript.

HTML structures the pages, CSS defines the visual style, Bootstrap helps with responsive layouts and navigation, and JavaScript powers destination search, category filters, favourites and story pop-ups.

Saved favourites use the browser's local storage, allowing the visitor to return to saved destinations on the same browser without needing an account or a database.
